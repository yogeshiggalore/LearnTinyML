# LearnTinyML - Course Naming

## Final Names

| Course | Name | Domain |
|--------|------|--------|
| Audio/Voice | **TinyVox** | Microphones, audio processing, voice |
| Vision/Camera | **TinyVue** | Cameras, image processing, computer vision |
| Motion/IMU | **TinyVibe** | Accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer |

## Why These Names?

### The "Tiny" Prefix
- Directly connects to **TinyML**, the umbrella brand of this project
- Reflects the microcontroller world — small, resource-constrained, efficient
- Consistent prefix creates a recognizable course family under LearnTinyML

### The "V" Pattern
All three names start with **V** — Vox, Vue, Vibe. This was intentional:
- Creates a **cohesive brand identity** across all three courses
- Easy to remember as a set
- Visually clean in logos, navigation, and documentation

### Individual Name Origins

#### TinyVox
- **Vox** is Latin for **"voice"**
- Widely recognized — used in words like "vox pop" (voice of the people), "vocal", "vocabulary"
- Represents the audio/voice domain: microphones, sound capture, speech, audio signals
- Short, punchy, and immediately conveys "sound/voice"

#### TinyVue
- **Vue** is French for **"view"** or **"vision"**
- Familiar to developers (Vue.js) and general audiences alike
- Represents the vision/camera domain: image sensors, pixel processing, visual perception
- Elegant spelling distinguishes it from generic "view"

#### TinyVibe
- **Vibe** is a common English shorthand for **"vibration"**
- Motion sensors (accelerometers, gyroscopes) fundamentally measure vibrations and movement
- Represents the motion/IMU domain: inertial sensing, orientation, gesture detection
- Natural, intuitive — everyone understands "vibe" as movement/feeling

## Naming Alternatives Considered

Several naming styles were evaluated before selecting the final set:

| Style | Audio | Vision | Motion | Why Not? |
|-------|-------|--------|--------|----------|
| Forge Series | SoundForge | PixelForge | MotionForge | SoundForge is an existing product |
| Pulse Series | SoundPulse | PixelPulse | MotionPulse | Too generic, no unifying pattern |
| Sense Series | HearSense | SeeSense | MoveSense | Awkward pronunciation (SeeSense) |
| Edge Series | EdgeEar | EdgeEye | EdgeMotion | Inconsistent word structure |
| Ear/Eye Series | TinyEar | TinyEye | TinyMotion | No unifying letter pattern |
| Nano Series | NanoSound | NanoVision | NanoMotion | "Nano" doesn't connect to TinyML |
| Decode Series | DecodeSound | DecodeLight | DecodeMotion | Too long, feels academic |
| Mic/Lens/Gyro | TinyMic | TinyLens | TinyGyro | Too hardware-specific, limits scope |

## Brand Hierarchy

```
LearnTinyML (Platform)
├── TinyVox  (Course 1: Audio/Voice)
├── TinyVue  (Course 2: Vision/Camera)
├── TinyVibe (Course 3: Motion/IMU)
└── TinyML   (Course 4: Machine Learning on Edge)
```

## Usage Guidelines

- Always capitalize as **TinyVox**, **TinyVue**, **TinyVibe** (CamelCase)
- In URLs and file paths, use lowercase: `tinyvox`, `tinyvue`, `tinyvibe`
- When referencing the full set, use: "the TinyV series" or "TinyVox/Vue/Vibe"
- Taglines for marketing/headers:
  - TinyVox — *"From Sound Waves to Smart Ears"*
  - TinyVue — *"From Photons to Perception"*
  - TinyVibe — *"From Movement to Meaning"*
